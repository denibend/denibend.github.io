Luxury became more market-sensitive after 2022 and less tied to Chinese equities, which peaked in 2020–22. Part of the rise in market beta may be mechanical: LVMH, Hermès and Richemont are large constituents of the STOXX 600 itself.

Reading tip: a step that reverses exactly two years later is one extreme week entering and then leaving the window, not a lasting change. The clearest case is March 2020, when the STOXX 600 fell 20% in a week.

  • Reactions are large only where the brand dominates group profit: Demna at Gucci and Tisci at Burberry. LVMH appointments (Louis Vuitton, Dior) barely register.
  • At Kering, CEO changes moved the stock more than most creative changes, but there are only four of them, at a company in turnaround.
  • Few results survive multiple testing. With 20 events and several windows, a handful of significant results is expected by chance; only the largest, such as Demna at |t| ≈ 6, are robust.

Data and method

  • Prices: Yahoo Finance via yfinance (adjusted close; raw data not redistributed). Indices: STOXX Europe 600, Hang Seng, iShares MSCI China ETF.
  • FX: ECB euro reference rates (USD, CHF, GBP, HKD); all stocks converted to EUR.
  • Returns: Friday-to-Friday log returns, which limit distortions from markets closing at different times; daily returns for the event study, aligned on common trading days.
  • Validation: no missing weeks; no stale prices; all ten daily moves above 15% checked against news; Yahoo's STOXX 600 lacks 64 trading days since 2014, handled by aligning prices before computing returns.
  • Events: each date verified against press coverage, with timing and confounders recorded.

Limitations

  • Associations, not causal effects. Weekly co-movement says nothing about fundamental exposure.
  • The European market index contains several of the stocks studied.
  • Brand-specific gaps are relative to peers, and the 2014–22 benchmark assumes stable sensitivities.
  • Event study: small sample, possible leaks before announcements, and same-day news (e.g. COVID on 24 Feb 2020; the Hang Seng crash on 8 Oct 2024).
