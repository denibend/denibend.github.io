Study 01 · Luxury research series · 23 October 2026

What drives European luxury stocks?

Market, currency and China exposure, creative-director and CEO changes, and the post-2023 divergence of eight European luxury groups, 2014–2026. Report and interactive dashboard.

How these studies are made

  • Every study starts from a stated question and hypotheses. A rejected hypothesis is reported as a result.
  • Public data only, with sources, retrieval dates and validation checks documented.
  • Analysis in Python (pandas, statsmodels), with automated tests of every statistical calculation.
  • Full source code, so every chart and number can be reproduced.
